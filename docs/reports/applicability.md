## 3. Decision

Limits: a coarse guard against a different sonar or rendering. It does not detect a subtle site or gain change inside the same sonar family, and a recording that passes is not proof that the promise holds there — only that the frames look like those it was fit on. Frames of one recording are correlated (the Clopper-Pearson bound assumes independence).
