# Sample sonar frames — attribution

These 8 side-scan sonar frames (and their YOLO labels) are taken, unmodified except for JPEG
re-encoding, from the **Ghost Pot Side-Scan Sonar Detection Dataset**
(PINGEcosystem, Hugging Face: `PINGEcosystem/sss-crab-pot-detection-ds`), licensed
**CC BY-SA 4.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).

Citation: Bodine, C. S., et al. (2026). *GhostVision: Democratizing Derelict Gear Detection Using
Low-Cost Sonar and Artificial Intelligence.* Journal of Marine Science and Engineering, 14(10), 951.
https://doi.org/10.3390/jmse14100951

They are redistributed here under the same license (ShareAlike). They carry **no GPS**; any map
shown for them in the DEPTH demo uses a clearly-labelled synthetic track.
