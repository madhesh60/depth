Timed study

Measures the minutes DEPTH saves instead of assuming them. You review half the frames by hand (click every pot) and the other half as DEPTH cards (real pot / not a pot). Every answer is timed; scoring is against the labels. ~5 minutes.

Order and frame halves rotate between people (2×2 Latin square), so nobody sees a frame twice and learning effects cancel out.

False-alarm audit

Are the detector's "false alarms" really false — or real objects nobody labelled? Judge each crop. It is blind: you never see the model's confidence, and some crops are known labelled pots (catch trials) that check the audit itself.
