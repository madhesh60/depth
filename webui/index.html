Measures the minutes DEPTH saves instead of assuming them. You review half the frames by hand (click every pot) and the other half as DEPTH cards (real pot / not a pot). Every answer is timed; scoring is against the labels. ~5 minutes.

Is it really a false alarm?

Each item is a spot the detector flagged on the calibration recordings. Most were not labelled as pots — but labellers miss things. For each crop decide what the bracketed return is:

  1. Real object — a pot, trap or other man-made thing (bright return, often a thin dark shadow beyond it, away from the top edge).
  2. Clutter / natural — rock, weed, seabed texture.
  3. Noise / artefact — speckle, stripes, water column.
  4. Unsure.

The result is the audited precision of the model: how many of its "false" alarms are real objects the labels missed. Enter your name on the left to start (you can stop and resume).

Does DEPTH save analyst time? Measure it.
